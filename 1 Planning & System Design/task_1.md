
## Task 1: Problem Statement

## Smart Recruitment System

### Problem Statement

Recruitment is a time-consuming and resource-intensive process, especially when organizations receive hundreds of resumes for a single job opening. Recruiters must manually review each resume, compare candidates against job requirements, and shortlist the most suitable applicants. This manual approach is not only slow but also increases the risk of overlooking qualified candidates due to human error and inconsistent evaluation.

The **Smart Recruitment System** aims to simplify and automate the recruitment process using Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Natural Language Processing (NLP). The platform enables recruiters to upload resumes and job descriptions, automatically extract candidate information, identify relevant skills, calculate job-match scores, rank applicants based on suitability, and manage the hiring process through an intuitive dashboard.

By reducing manual effort, improving candidate evaluation accuracy, and providing data-driven insights, the system helps recruiters make faster and more informed hiring decisions while delivering a better experience for both recruiters and job applicants.

---

### Objective

The primary objective of this project is to build a production-ready AI-powered recruitment platform that integrates Django, Django REST Framework, Next.js, PostgreSQL, and AI/NLP techniques into a modern Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) application.

The platform will provide secure authentication, resume analysis, candidate ranking, recruiter dashboards, and AI-powered job matching while following clean architecture, scalable design principles, and deployment best practices.
